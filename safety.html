Monsoon Landslide Preparedness

During heavy rains (June–September), NH-29 (Dimapur–Kohima) is susceptible to mudslides. Verify route clearance at the Chumoukedima checkpost before departing.

Hiring Certified Village Guides

Trail markings inside Dzükou Valley and Japfü can become invisible under dense mountain fog. Always take a registered local guide from SAYO (Southern Angami Youth Org) at Viswema or Jakhama.

Cash Reliance in Interior Belts

While Kohima and Dimapur widely accept UPI, ATM machines frequently run dry in interior towns (Phek, Mon, Wokha). Keep sufficient cash reserves for sumos, entry fees, and homestays.

Early Sundown Schedule

Due to the eastern longitude, daylight fades by 4:30 PM–5:00 PM during autumn and winter. Start overland transit early in the morning and reach your destination before dusk.
