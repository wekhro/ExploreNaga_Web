Monsoon & Landslide Precautions

During heavy monsoon periods (June to September), sections along NH-29 (Dimapur–Kohima) can experience rockfalls. Check local taxi stands for route clearance before departing.

Offline Preparation

Cellular data (Jio/Airtel) may drop along remote stretches such as Doyang reservoir, upper Mon, and Pfütsero valleys. Download offline maps and keep cash on hand, as rural ATMs frequently run dry.

Night Driving

Avoid highway transit between districts after 7:00 PM due to heavy hill fog, lack of streetlights, and narrow single-lane mountain passes.
