Permit & Registration

Foreign passport holders must submit registration within 24 hours of arrival in Nagaland.

FRO Filing Requirements:

• Scanned Passport photo page & Indian visa stamp.

• Citizens of China, Pakistan, and Afghanistan require pre-approved MHA clearance.

ILP Filing Requirements:

• Government photo identity card scan.

• One passport-size photo (white background).

Entering your approved reference adds a verified badge to your dashboard and registers this permit to your My Documents folder.
