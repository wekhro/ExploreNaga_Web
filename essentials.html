Respect Customary Guidelines

Nagaland's tribal communities hold customary land rights and village autonomy. Following local etiquette ensures a welcoming and respectful journey.

Recommended Practices (Do's)
Village Gate Courtesy & Council Fees
Heritage villages like Khonoma or Kigwema maintain nominal entry fees managed by their Village Development Board. Keep physical cash ready at village gates.
Hire Registered Local Guides
For high-altitude valley routes like Dzükou or sacred forests, always recruit a guide certified by the village youth organization (e.g., SAYO) to support community livelihoods and stay on safe trails.
Seek Consent Before Photography
Always request permission before taking photos of elders, village residents, ceremonial morungs, or inside tribal family kitchens.
Plan Around Sunday Observance
Sunday is strictly observed as a day of worship and rest. Commercial shops, private transport sumos, and local markets remain closed. Plan long-distance travel for other days.
Strict Restrictions (Don'ts)
Zero-Plastic Policy: Dzükou Valley and Khonoma Nature Conservation areas are strict plastic-free zones. Carry all wrappers back to the roadhead.
Flora Extraction: Never pluck the endemic Dzükou Lily or wild orchids from mountain ridges; violations carry severe fines from village councils.
Indian Citizens

Inner Line Permit (ILP) Requirement

Under the Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation Act of 1873, all non-resident Indian citizens require a valid ILP to enter Nagaland for tourism or transit.

Application Overview:

Official Online Portal

Apply via the official Nagaland portal: ilp.nagaland.gov.in

Required Documents

• Passport photo • Valid Govt Photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport) • Local hotel or tour operator contact.

Turnaround Time & Fees

Tourist pass: ₹50 – ₹100. Approval turnaround is typically within 24 to 48 hours.

International Travelers

Foreigner Registration Requirements

Foreign nationals no longer require a Protected Area Permit (PAP) for most of Nagaland, but mandatory online or local police desk registration remains in effect.

24-Hour Registration Rule

Foreign passport holders must register within 24 hours of arrival at the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRO/e-FRRO) or through the registration register at licensed homestays and hotels.

Restricted Nationalities Notice

Citizens of China, Pakistan, and Afghanistan require prior clearance from the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India, before entry.
