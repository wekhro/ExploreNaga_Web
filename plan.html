Curated multi-day itineraries across Nagaland:

4 Days / 3 Nights Moderate Trek

Kohima & Dzükou Valley Circuit

Dimapur ➔ Kohima Heritage ➔ Khonoma Green Village ➔ Dzükou Trek Base

• Day 1: Dimapur to Kohima WWII Cemetery & night bazaar.

• Day 2: Angami culture & bastion walk in Khonoma village.

• Day 3-4: Trek up via Viswema, camp at Dzükou, exit via Jakhama.

5 Days / 4 Nights Cultural Immersion

Mon & Konyak Borderland Circuit

Dimapur ➔ Sonari / Mon Town ➔ Longwa Village ➔ Hongphoi

• Day 1-2: Journey to Mon; visit local brass and wood artisans.

• Day 3: Stay in Longwa; cross-border chief's morung house.

• Day 4-5: Traditional Morungs in Hongphoi & return transit.

Major cultural festivals and tribal harvest celebrations:

State Festival Dec 1 – Dec 10

Hornbill Festival

Location: Kisama Heritage Village (12 km south of Kohima).
Brings together all recognized Naga tribes for cultural dance, folk music, traditional sports, and indigenous crafts.

💡 Planning Tip: Homestays and shared sumos fill up months in advance. Secure bookings early.

Sekrenyi (Angami Tribe)

February

Purification festival marked by bridge renovations, community feasts, and singing in Kohima district.

Aoleang (Konyak Tribe)

April 1 – 6

Welcomes spring and prays for a bountiful harvest in Mon district with celebratory dance and feasts.

Moatsü (Ao Tribe)

May 1 – 3

Celebrated across Mokokchung after seed-sowing with tug-of-war competitions and log-drum rhythms.
