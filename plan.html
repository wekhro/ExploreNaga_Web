Major Event Dec 1 – Dec 10

Hornbill Festival (Festival of Festivals)

Staged annually at the Naga Heritage Village in Kisama (12 km south of Kohima). Unites all recognized tribes featuring folk dances, war cries, craft markets, wrestling, and indigenous music.

Lodging Advisory: Homestays and hotels in Kohima book out 4–6 months in advance. Secure permits and reservations early.

Community accommodation and verified trek assistance:

Village Homestays

The most authentic way to experience Nagaland. Village elders and youth tourism committees manage regulated homestays serving farm-fresh Naga organic food.

• Khonoma: Co-ordinated by KNCTS Eco-Tourism Board
• Kigwema & Jakhama: Ideal trail bases for Dzükou hikes
• Average Rates: ₹1,200 – ₹2,500 per night (includes dinner & tea)

Certified Local Guides
