Travel Essentials

Mandatory travel documents & registration protocols:

Indian Citizens ₹50 – ₹500

Inner Line Permit (ILP)

Required under the Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation 1873 for all domestic tourists entering Nagaland (including Dimapur).

Documents: Aadhaar / Voter ID + Passport photo
Turnaround: 24–48 hours for e-ILP approval
Apply on Official Portal
International Tourists Free Registration

Foreigner Registration (FRO)

Foreign passport holders do not require a PAP (Protected Area Permit) for standard circuits, but must register with the local district FRO within 24 hours of arrival.

Exclusions: Passport holders from Pakistan, China, and Afghanistan require prior approval from the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA).

Village Councils & Traditions

Every village in Nagaland is governed by its customary council. Respecting local norms ensures safety and genuine hospitality.

📸

Consent for Photography

Always request permission before taking photos of village elders, traditional morung structures, and private courtyards.

⛪

Sunday Observance

Most shops, vehicle counters, and eateries remain closed on Sundays for prayer. Stock up on fuel and essentials on Saturday.

🚯

Zero-Tolerance for Littering

Villages like Khonoma enforce strict penalties (up to ₹5,000 fines) for discarding single-use plastics.

🤝

Receiving Food & Gifts

When offered traditional black tea or snacks in a village home, receive the cup using both hands as a gesture of respect.
