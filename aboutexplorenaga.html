ExploreNaga 🌿

Stay connected, travel safely across the hill districts of Nagaland, India.

Our Mission

ExploreNaga is built to bridge remote hill connectivity with responsible cultural exploration. We empower travelers with verified transport fare benchmarks, Inner Line Permit guidance, and reliable safety check-ins while preserving local tribal autonomy and ecology.

What We Provide

Traveler Safety Hub

Offline check-ins, local police directories, and rapid emergency triggers for trail rescues (like Dzükou SAYO teams).

Fair Transit Transparency

Standardized fare benchmarks for shared Sumos, local town taxis, and NST bus routes so travelers never get overcharged.

Eco-Tourism & Ethics

Promoting sustainable community-run homestays and raising awareness around village council bylaws and plastic prohibitions.

The Traveler's Pledge

  • Always take back non-biodegradable waste from trails and campsites.
  • Ask permission before photographing residents, elders, and Morungs.
  • Respect Sunday church hours and village rest days across districts.
